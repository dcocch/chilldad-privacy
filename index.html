Chill Dad – Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Data Storage

All check-ins, notes, kids' information, and personal data are stored locally on your device and backed up to your personal iCloud account. We do not operate any servers and have no access to your data.

Data We Do Not Collect

Chill Dad does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. We have no analytics, no crash reporting, and no usage tracking. We do not know who you are.

AI Features

Chill Dad uses OpenAI's API for two features:

Parenting Support

When you submit a question in the parenting support feature, your question is sent to OpenAI to generate a response. These requests are processed in real time and are not stored on any server we control.

Note Analysis

When you include a written note in your daily check-in, that note text and your score for the day are sent to OpenAI's API for background analysis. This is used to identify recurring topics and patterns in your Insights. No other personal information is included in this request. Results are stored locally on your device only.

Please review OpenAI's privacy policy at openai.com/privacy for their data practices.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, they are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data leaves your device.

Children's Privacy

Chill Dad is designed for use by adults. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.
